# 🌱 CSR & Sustainability Assessment – Souls of Humanity

### ESG, SDG & Social Impact Analysis of a Non-Profit Organization

This academic project presents a comprehensive **CSR and sustainability assessment of Souls of Humanity**, a grassroots non-profit organization, using **ESG, SDG, Stakeholder Analysis, Triple Bottom Line, and Financial Analysis frameworks**.

The study evaluates the organization's social impact, resource utilization, governance practices, sustainability initiatives, financial position, stakeholder relationships, and opportunities for strengthening long-term organizational sustainability.

> **Academic Team Project | CSR & Sustainability | GITAM School of Business | Team 3**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Souls of Humanity is a grassroots non-profit organization working across areas including:

- Education
- Healthcare
- Rural development
- Food support
- Environmental sustainability
- Women empowerment
- Sports
- Disaster relief

The project examines how these initiatives contribute to social and environmental impact while evaluating the organization's operational and governance challenges.

The assessment combines qualitative observations with available financial and impact data to understand how the organization creates and sustains social value.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The key objectives of the project were to:

- Understand the CSR and sustainability practices of Souls of Humanity
- Analyse the organization's major social-impact initiatives
- Evaluate financial performance and resource utilization
- Identify and analyse key stakeholders
- Apply the Triple Bottom Line framework
- Evaluate Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) dimensions
- Map organizational initiatives to relevant Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
- Identify gaps in impact measurement and governance
- Develop recommendations and an action plan for improved sustainability

---

## 🏢 Organization Overview

Souls of Humanity was founded in **2018** and registered in **2019**.

The organization follows a grassroots, community-oriented approach and operates across multiple social-impact areas.

Its activities focus on addressing issues such as poverty, social inequality, education access, healthcare access, environmental sustainability, women empowerment, and emergency support.

The organization's approach emphasizes community engagement and maximizing social impact through relatively lean resource utilization.

---

## 🌍 Major CSR Initiatives

The study examined several major initiatives undertaken by Souls of Humanity.

### 📚 Education – Mission Vidya Vikas

The initiative focuses on improving access to education for underserved children.

Activities include:

- Identification of educational gaps
- Selection of beneficiaries
- Volunteer mobilization
- Distribution of educational resources
- Student participation
- Monitoring and continuation

The initiative was assessed in relation to **SDG 4 – Quality Education**.

---

### 🌾 Rural Development

Rural development initiatives focus on improving living conditions in underserved communities through:

- Education
- Healthcare
- Food support
- Clean water
- Sanitation
- Basic infrastructure
- Women empowerment
- Skill development

The project also examined a **stitching workshop initiative** designed to provide rural women with income-generating skills.

---

### 🍲 Food & Social Support

Food and support initiatives provide assistance to vulnerable communities through:

- Food distribution
- Clothing distribution
- Basic goods
- Community support activities
- Support for disadvantaged children

The report identifies alignment with:

- **SDG 1 – No Poverty**
- **SDG 2 – Zero Hunger**
- **SDG 10 – Reduced Inequalities**
- **SDG 4 – Quality Education**

---

### 🏥 Healthcare Initiatives

Healthcare activities include:

- Medical camps
- Health screening
- Basic healthcare services
- Medicine distribution
- Eye-care camps
- Dental camps
- Blood donation drives
- Emergency healthcare support

The report notes medical camps reaching approximately **100 beneficiaries**, specialized camps reaching around **30–35 individuals**, and blood donation drives involving approximately **40–50 donors**.

The activities were primarily aligned with **SDG 3 – Good Health and Well-being**.

---

### ♀️ Women Empowerment

Women empowerment initiatives include programs focused on:

- Menstrual hygiene awareness
- Self-defence
- Health awareness
- Confidence building
- Personal safety
- Skill development

The initiatives were assessed primarily against:

- **SDG 5 – Gender Equality**
- **SDG 3 – Good Health and Well-being**
- **SDG 4 – Quality Education**

---

### 🌱 Environmental Initiatives

Environmental activities examined in the study include:

- Beach clean-up activities
- Plantation programs
- Plastic collection
- Recycling
- Environmental awareness
- Community participation

The report notes approximately **80 kg of plastic collected per drive** and approximately **2,000 plants provided to villages**.

Recycling-related revenue is also reinvested into other initiatives, supporting a resource-efficient approach.

---

### 🚨 Disaster & Other Social Support

The organization also provides emergency and humanitarian support during situations such as:

- Pandemics
- Floods
- Accidents
- Extreme weather

Support can include:

- Food
- Medicines
- Oxygen concentrators
- Other essential supplies

Additional activities include food banks, clothing drives, sanitation awareness, and animal feeding.

## 💰 Financial Analysis

The project analysed the organization's financial position using available financial information.

### FY 2023–24

| Metric | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Total Income | ₹16.85 Lakhs |
| Total Expenditure | ₹25.01 Lakhs |
| Reported Deficit | ₹8.15 Lakhs |

The analysis identifies a deficit-based operating model in which expenditure exceeds income.

The report also notes that part of the annual deficit is associated with investment in a vehicle intended to strengthen the organization's long-term operating capacity.

---

### January 2026

| Metric | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Contributions | ₹1.61 Lakhs |
| Expenditure | ₹1.87 Lakhs |
| Monthly Deficit | ₹26,100 |
| Beneficiaries | 2,160 |
| Volunteer Hours | 556 |

Approximately **95% of January 2026 contributions were from non-member sources**, indicating significant reliance on external donations.

Despite financial constraints, the organization generated substantial community reach using volunteer participation and relatively lean resources.
